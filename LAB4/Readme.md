## 🧩 Problem Set Overview

| Task | Algorithmic Topic | Technical Highlight & Complexity
| :---: | :--- | :--- 
| **[Q1](./Q1)** | **Application I: Stable Chromatic Sort** | Utilizes counting sort mechanics to group elements in $O(n)$ time while strictly preserving numerical stability. 
| **[Q2](./Q2)** | **Application II: Target Sum Across Dual Sets** | Leverages an efficient two-pointer scan over sorted arrays to find pairs satisfying $a + b = x$ in $O(n \log n)$.
| **[Q3](./Q3)** | **Application III: The Generalized $k$-Sum Challenge** | Combines recursive subset selection with binary search to evaluate $k$-sums efficiently in $O(n^{k-1} \log n)$. 
| **[Q4](./Q4)** | **Application IV: Peak Concurrent Attendees** | Employs an event-driven sweep-line technique to compute the maximum simultaneous overlaps in $O(n \log n)$. 
| **[Q5](./Q5)** | **Application V: Consolidating Overlapping Intervals** | Sorts elements by initialization times to seamlessly fuse contiguous and overlapping intervals in $O(n \log n)$. 
| **[Q6](./Q6)** | **Application VI: Max Interval Intersection Point** | Applies a refined sweep-line approach with inclusive endpoint tie-breaking to find the highest density coordinate in $O(n \log n)$.
